How you'll be graded

  • Correctness — 40%. Is the problem actually solved, at the root cause? Automated checks decide this.
  • Engineering quality — 25%. A surgical fix that follows the codebase's conventions, with no collateral damage.
  • Documentation — 25%. Comments where they are needed, docstrings, commit messages that say why, and a filled-in NOTES.md.
  • Completeness — 10%. Every item in the definition of done.

Result is a letter grade from F to A+. An unfixed problem is capped at C+ no matter how well documented; a silent fix cannot reach an A. Running out of time is not an automatic fail — partial work with an honest writeup still scores. Editing the tests to make them pass is an automatic F.
