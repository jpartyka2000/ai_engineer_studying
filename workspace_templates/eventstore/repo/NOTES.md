# Notes

Working notes. Replace this with yours.

---

## What I changed

_One or two sentences. What is different now that was not before._

## Root cause

_What was actually wrong, not what the symptom was. If you are implementing something
rather than fixing it, say what the gap was and why the obvious implementation is not
the right one._

## How I found it

_The path you actually took, including the things that turned out to be dead ends.
`git log`, a failing test, an explain plan, a number that did not add up._

## Why this fix rather than another

_What else you considered. If you ruled something out, say what ruled it out._

## Blast radius

_What else is affected. Which numbers that were already reported are now wrong, and
whether anything needs reissuing or backfilling. For a data pipeline this is usually the
most important section and the one most often left empty._

## What I would do next with more time

_Monitoring, a test that would have caught this, a design change you did not have room
for._
