## What I changed

_One or two sentences. What is different now that was not before._

## Root cause

_What was actually wrong, not what the symptom was. For a retrieval problem, say which
stage produced the wrong result and how you narrowed it to that stage rather than the one
either side of it._

## How I found it

_The path you actually took, including the dead ends. A failing test, a metric that
moved, `make evald` output, a question that started being refused._

## What the metrics said, before and after

_Numbers. Which of the four moved, by how much, and whether any stayed still that you
expected to move — that last one is usually the interesting part._

## Why this fix rather than another

_What else you considered and what ruled it out._

## What I would do next with more time

_The test that would have caught this, the number that should have been on a dashboard,
the design change you did not have room for._
