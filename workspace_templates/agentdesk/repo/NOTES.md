## What I changed

_One or two sentences. What is different now that was not before._

## Root cause

_What was actually wrong, not what the symptom was. For an agent problem, say which
round of the loop first went wrong and how you ruled out the rounds either side of it.
For a latency problem, name the resource -- a pooled connection, the event loop thread,
a model call per row -- rather than saying it was slow._

## How I found it

_The path you actually took, including the dead ends. A failing test, `make ask`, a
trace, `make bench` before and after._

## Why this fix rather than another

_What else you considered and what ruled it out._

## What I did not change, and why

_Anything you noticed, decided to leave, and have a reason for. This is worth as much as
the fix: a diff that touches only what it needs is the point._
