## What I changed

_One or two sentences. What is different now that was not before._

## Root cause

_What was actually wrong, not what the symptom was. For an isolation bug, say which
layer failed and why the other one did not catch it._

## How I found it

_The path you actually took, including the dead ends. `git log`, a failing test, an
execution plan, a row count that was exactly double what it should have been._

## Blast radius

_Who could have read whose data, through which code path, and for how long. If you can
bound it from the audit trail or from `git log`, do — "we cannot tell" is also an answer,
and an important one._

## Why this fix rather than another

_What else you considered and what ruled it out. If your fix is a general traversal
rather than a special case, say why that distinction matters here._

## What I would do next with more time

_The test that would have caught this, the monitoring that would have surfaced it, the
design change you did not have room for._
